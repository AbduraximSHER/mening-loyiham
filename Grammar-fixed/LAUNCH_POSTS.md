## Product Hunt

**Tagline:** 100 advanced English grammar units. No account. No ads. Works offline.

**Description:**
GrammarHub is a free, offline-capable English grammar practice site for advanced learners.

**100 units** covering every major grammar point at C1/C2 level — tenses, modals, passives, reported speech, articles, agreement, verb patterns, and vocabulary.

**1,100+ exercises** across four types: multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, error correction (you fix broken sentences), and word reordering.

**What's different:**
→ Mastery threshold: you need 70% to mark a unit complete — no gaming the system
→ Per-exercise spaced repetition: wrong answers resurface after 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days  
→ Works offline as a PWA — install to your home screen
→ No account required — progress lives on your device with backup/restore
→ Free forever for all 100 grammar units

Built for IELTS/TOEFL/Cambridge exam preparation and for anyone who wants to close gaps in near-native English grammar.
